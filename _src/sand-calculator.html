How to use the sand calculator

  1. Measure the area and pick the matching shape — a circle for a round pool base, a rectangle for a patio or sandbox.
  2. Enter the depth of sand you need. Under pavers that's the bedding layer only, not the gravel base below it.
  3. Choose dry or damp sand. Damp sand weighs noticeably more, which matters if you're ordering by the ton or hauling it yourself.

How to calculate sand

Volume (ft³) = area (ft²) × depth (in) ÷ 12 Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27 Tons = cubic yards × density (≈1.35 dry, ≈1.6 damp) 50 lb bags = cubic feet ÷ 0.5 (round up)

Worked example: bedding sand for a 12 × 12 ft paver patio

  1. Area: 144 sq ft; depth 1 inch
  2. Volume: 144 × 1 ÷ 12 = 12 ft³ = 0.44 yd³
  3. Plus 10%: 0.49 yd³ — about 0.66 tons, or 27 bags of 50 lb.

Worked example: a 5 × 5 ft sandbox filled 8 inches deep

  1. Volume: 25 × 8 ÷ 12 = 16.7 ft³ = 0.62 yd³
  2. That's 34 bags of 50 lb of play sand, or about 0.8 tons in bulk.

How much does a yard of sand weigh?

A cubic yard of dry sand weighs roughly 2,600 to 2,800 lb (about 1.3 to 1.4 tons). Wet sand holds water between the grains and can weigh 3,000 lb or more. If you're hauling sand in a pickup, check its payload: a half-ton truck may only carry about half a yard safely.

Which sand for which job?

For pavers, the sand bedding sits on a compacted gravel base — see how to build a paver patio base for the layers and depths.

Don't use sand to fix a bad base. Under pavers, sand is a thin, even bedding layer. Deep sand shifts and ruts; level the compacted gravel base first and keep the sand close to an inch.

Frequently asked questions

How many 50 lb bags of sand are in a yard?

About 54, since a 50 lb bag holds roughly half a cubic foot and a yard is 27 cubic feet. Check the bag: some list a slightly different volume.

How much sand do I need under pavers?

About 1 inch over the gravel base. For 100 square feet, that's roughly 8.3 cubic feet — 0.31 cubic yards or 17 bags of 50 lb.

Is a ton of sand the same as a yard?

No. A yard of dry sand weighs about 1.35 tons, so one ton is roughly 0.75 cubic yards.

How much sand for an above-ground pool?

For a 24 ft round pool with a 2-inch base, the area is about 452 sq ft, so you need roughly 75 cubic feet — close to 2.8 cubic yards.
