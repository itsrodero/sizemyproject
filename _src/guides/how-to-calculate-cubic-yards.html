The formula

A cubic yard is a cube 3 feet on each side: 3 × 3 × 3 = 27 cubic feet. So:

Cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (ft) ÷ 27

The only trap is units. Depth is usually measured in inches, so convert it to feet by dividing by 12 before you multiply.

A shortcut for area × inches

If you already know the area in square feet and the depth in inches:

Cubic yards = square feet × inches ÷ 324

That's because 12 × 27 = 324. It also means one cubic yard covers 324 square feet at 1 inch deep, 162 at 2 inches, 108 at 3 inches and 81 at 4 inches.

Worked examples

A 10 × 12 ft patio slab, 4 inches thick

  1. 10 × 12 × 0.333 = 40 ft³
  2. 40 ÷ 27 = 1.48 cubic yards

A flower bed of 150 sq ft, 3 inches of mulch

  1. 150 × 3 ÷ 324 = 1.39 cubic yards

A round fire pit area 14 ft across, 3 inches of pea gravel

  1. Area: π × 7² = 153.9 sq ft
  2. 153.9 × 3 ÷ 324 = 1.43 cubic yards

Odd shapes

Split the area into rectangles, triangles and circles, work out each area and add them up — then multiply by the depth. The square footage calculator adds shapes for you, and how to measure an irregular area covers curves.

Metric

Cubic meters = length (m) × width (m) × depth (m) 1 cubic yard = 0.7646 m³ 1 m³ = 1.308 yd³

From cubic yards to what you buy

  • Concrete: ready-mix is ordered in cubic yards; bags are counted by yield (45 bags of 80 lb per yard) — use the concrete calculator.
  • Gravel and sand: often sold by the ton — multiply yards by the density (about 1.4 for crushed stone). See cubic yards to tons.
  • Mulch and soil in bags: divide cubic feet by the bag size (a yard is 13.5 bags of 2 cu ft).
  • Always add a margin — 5 to 10% for waste, settling and uneven ground.

Frequently asked questions

How many cubic feet are in a cubic yard?

27.

How many square feet does a cubic yard cover?

324 ÷ depth in inches: 108 sq ft at 3 inches, 81 sq ft at 4 inches.

How do I convert cubic feet to cubic yards?

Divide by 27. For example, 54 cubic feet is 2 cubic yards.
