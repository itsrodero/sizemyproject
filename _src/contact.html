What to include

  • Reporting a wrong result? Send the link to the calculator with your inputs (use the Copy link button) and what you expected to see.
  • Suggesting a calculator? Tell us what you're building and what you'd need to buy.
  • Have a better source? Links to manufacturer data sheets or official guidance are especially welcome.

We can't give project-specific engineering advice or recommend contractors. For structural questions, please talk to a local professional or your building department.
