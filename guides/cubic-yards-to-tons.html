Cubic yards to tons: how to convert landscape materials

Landscape yards sell by the cubic yard, quarries by the ton — and the conversion depends on the material. Here's how to switch between the two without guessing.

By Daniel · Updated September 30, 2026 · 3 min read · How we calculate

  2. Conversion chart
  3. Worked examples

Yards measure space, tons measure weight

A cubic yard is a volume: a cube 3 feet on each side, or 27 cubic feet. A ton (in the US, a short ton) is a weight: 2,000 pounds. To go from one to the other you need the material's density — how much one cubic yard of it weighs.

Tons = cubic yards × tons per cubic yard Cubic yards = tons ÷ tons per cubic yard

Worked examples

How many tons is 5 cubic yards of crushed stone?

  1. 5 yd³ × 1.4 t/yd³ = 7 tons (up to 8.5 tons for heavier stone)

How many cubic yards is a 10-ton load of sand?

  1. 10 t ÷ 1.35 t/yd³ = 7.4 yd³ of dry sand; if it's wet, closer to 6.3 yd³

How many tons of gravel for a 10 × 20 ft patio base, 4 inches deep?

  1. Volume: 10 × 20 × 4 ÷ 12 = 66.7 ft³ = 2.47 yd³
  2. Weight: 2.47 × 1.4 = 3.5 tons, or about 3.8 tons with 10% for compaction

Why the same yard weighs more or less

  • Moisture: water fills the gaps between grains. Wet sand and soil can weigh 20% or more than the same volume dry — and you pay for that water when you buy by the ton.
  • Shape: angular crushed stone locks together more tightly than round pebbles, so more rock fits in a yard.
  • Fines: blends with dust and small particles (crusher run) fill gaps and weigh more than clean, washed stone of a single size.
  • Compaction: material in the truck is loose. Once compacted it takes up less space, which is why calculators add around 10%.

Checking your truck or trailer

Weight matters when you haul it yourself. A cubic yard of gravel weighs roughly 2,800 lb or more — more than many half-ton pickups can carry safely. Check the payload on the door-jamb sticker, and remember that a trailer also has a maximum load and a tongue weight limit.

Frequently asked questions

Is a ton of gravel more than a yard?

No, less. A cubic yard of crushed stone weighs about 1.4 tons, so one ton is roughly 0.7 cubic yards.

How many cubic feet are in a ton of gravel?

About 19 cubic feet (0.7 yd³ × 27), at 1.4 tons per yard.

Should I buy by the yard or by the ton?

Whichever your supplier uses — just convert both quotes to the same unit to compare. When buying wet material by the ton, you're paying partly for water.
